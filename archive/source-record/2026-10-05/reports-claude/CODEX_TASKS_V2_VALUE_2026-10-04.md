Our engine runs underneath: (1) detects a recurring problem in real data, (2) reasons with Scout / independent Verifier / Builder, (3) sends a proposal to agent-core as a draft (a better prompt, a link to an existing tool, or a new specialist agent), (4) a human sees it in the platform with its evidence, evaluates, approves, (5) the release happens, (6) the engine observes whether it worked and says so honestly. ## T1. Outcome estimator validated by placebo (closes step 6: "did it work?")

Question: after a release changes an agent, how do we know the problem rate moved because of it? Build `docs/data/outcome/` (or your usual pack path) with a deterministic Python (or Rust, your choice) estimator and its validation on the REAL bank cell tables (37 months, `call_center_interactions`, `complaints`, `satisfaction_surveys`):
- Design: pre/post on a treated cell (e.g. reason x channel) against same-period control cells (same channel, other reasons; and a second control by customer-hash half), difference-in-differences on proportions, with interval, minimum support, minimum observation window rule, and a pre-registered decision vocabulary: `improved`, `no_detectable_change`, `worsened`, `inconclusive` (never "caused").
- Validation that does NOT need a real release: (a) placebo: pick random fake release dates on the 37 months; the estimator must report `no_detectable_change`/`inconclusive` at the pre-registered false-positive rate (state it, e.g. <=5%); (b) power: inject a known absolute reduction (2, 5, 10 pp) into the post period of the real aggregates and report the detection rate and the minimum detectable effect per cell size (n per 30 days). Report which cells (channels) are too small to ever detect an effect (earlier audit: only Phone cells reach n>=500 per 30 days): the engine must say `inconclusive: underpowered` instead of claiming success.
- Deliver: the estimator as a pure function over cell tables (same `ndjson` cell-table schema as `scripts/aggregate/bank_cells.py` on main: read it, adopt it exactly), a README, the placebo/power report, and a test suite. Acceptance: byte-identical reruns, k>=10 scan test, the false-positive rate measured and below the stated bound, power table published.
